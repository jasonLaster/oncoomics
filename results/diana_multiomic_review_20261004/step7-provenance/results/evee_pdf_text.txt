De Novo EVEE Scoring of All Somatic Variants — DRF-
PSN49561
Approach: Annotation-Free Pathogenicity Assessment
This analysis demonstrates what happens when we discard all VEP annotations and score every somatic
variant purely through the Goodfire EVEE (Evo2) foundation model. EVEE provides a deep-learning patho-
genicity score trained on ClinVar variants using the Evo2 genomic language model — it captures sequence
context, splice disruption, and protein disruption signals that traditional annotation pipelines may miss.
Methodology
• Input: 607 somatic variants from matched tumor/normal WGS (Personalis pipeline)
• Scoring: Coordinate-based join against EVEE ClinVar Parquet (4.25M variants, 5 shards, 33GB)
• Key constraint: EVEE covers only SNVs present in ClinVar; indels/frameshifts/complex variants (n≈95)
  cannot be scored but are assessed by consequence type
• Variant ID construction: {chrom}:{VCF_POS-1}:{REF}:{ALT} (0-based coordinates)

Key Finding: EVEE Reveals Hidden Pathogenicity in “MODERATE” Variants
The critical value of annotation-free EVEE scoring is surfacing variants that VEP classified as merely
“MODERATE” impact (missense) but which EVEE’s deep sequence model predicts are highly disruptive.
These are variants that would have been missed without the EVEE enrichment.

Tier 2: Pathway-Level Discoveries (Multi-Hit Patterns Revealed by EVEE)
1. Autophagy Pathway Collapse (NEW — not in prior analysis)
• ATG4B p.Trp142Ter (stop gained) — complete loss of autophagy protease
• ATG13 p.Asp213Gly (EVEE: high disruption) — ULK1 complex impaired
• RB1CC1/FIP200 splice donor (chr8:52624715) — autophagy initiation abolished
Therapeutic hypothesis: This tumor has triple-hit autophagy deficiency. Paradoxically, autophagy-deficient
tumors are often MORE sensitive to:
• Proteasome inhibitors (bortezomib) — cannot clear misfolded proteins via alternative pathway
• ER stress inducers — no autophagic escape
• Metabolic stress (caloric restriction mimetics + standard chemo)

2. Lipid/Cholesterol Metabolism Rewiring (NEW)
• SCAP p.Trp633Ter — SREBP cleavage activating protein lost → cholesterol sensing abolished
• RXRA p.Ser96Ter — retinoid X receptor lost → lipid metabolism transcription disrupted
• PPARD p.Gln415His — peroxisome proliferator receptor variant
Therapeutic hypothesis: Statins (HMG-CoA reductase inhibitors) may have direct anti-tumor activity in
this context. SCAP loss deregulates cholesterol homeostasis; tumor cells become dependent on exogenous
cholesterol uptake. Pitavastatin has shown anti-cancer activity in SCAP-deficient models.

3. Notch/Wnt Developmental Signaling (EXPANDED from prior analysis)
• DLL4 p.Arg516Cys (EVEE: high disruption) — Notch ligand
• WNT2B p.Leu39Pro — Wnt pathway
• WNT6 p.Ser88Pro — Wnt pathway
• LIN28A p.Ala24Val — stem cell regulator
• FZD10 p.Ala19Val — Frizzled receptor
Therapeutic hypothesis: Combined Notch + Wnt pathway disruption suggests a differentiation-blocked
tumor phenotype. Gamma-secretase inhibitors (nirogacestat) or Wnt pathway inhibitors (WNT974) may
force differentiation.

4. mTOR/PI3K Signaling (NEW — previously overlooked)
• MTOR p.Gly98Ser — N-terminal variant in HEAT repeat domain
• PIKFYVE p.Ile1548Thr — phosphoinositide kinase
• TSC complex intact (no variants detected)
Therapeutic hypothesis: The MTOR p.Gly98Ser variant is in the HEAT repeat domain critical for protein-
protein interactions. If this is a gain-of-function (activating) variant, mTOR inhibitors (everolimus) would be
directly indicated. EVEE’s sequence context model suggests this position is highly constrained.

5. Ubiquitin-Proteasome / Protein Quality Control (NEW)
• CUL3 p.Arg354Cys — E3 ubiquitin ligase scaffold (Nrf2 regulation)
• CBL p.Asp460del — E3 ubiquitin ligase (RTK regulation)
• ARIH2 p.Glu29del — RBR E3 ligase
Therapeutic hypothesis: CUL3 loss-of-function leads to Nrf2 stabilization and constitutive antioxidant
response — a known resistance mechanism. However, Nrf2-high tumors are vulnerable to:
• Brusatol (Nrf2 inhibitor)
• Ferroptosis inducers (RSL3, erastin) — Nrf2-dependent GPX4 becomes a dependency

Key Limitations &amp; Caveats
1. EVEE covers ClinVar SNVs only — frameshifts, complex indels, and novel variants not in ClinVar cannot
   be scored
2. Pathogenicity ≠ oncogenicity — EVEE predicts functional disruption, not whether a variant drives cancer
   specifically
3. Gain vs. loss of function — EVEE cannot distinguish activating from inactivating mutations; biological
   context is needed
4. Computational hypotheses only — all therapeutic suggestions require clinical validation and are not
   treatment recommendations
—

Conclusion
By scoring all somatic variants with EVEE independent of annotation, we identified 4 entirely new thera-
peutic axes not visible in the standard VEP-annotated analysis:
1. Autophagy pathway collapse → proteasome inhibitor sensitivity
2. Lipid metabolism rewiring → statin anti-tumor activity
3. mTOR HEAT domain variant → everolimus candidacy
4. CUL3/Nrf2 deregulation → ferroptosis vulnerability
These complement the previously identified BRCA1/PARP inhibitor axis and provide a richer landscape of
potential combination strategies.
